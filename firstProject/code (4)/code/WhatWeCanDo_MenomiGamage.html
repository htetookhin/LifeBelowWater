Parlimentaries for Global Action

Oceans play a vital role in sustaining life on Earth—regulating climate, supporting biodiversity, and providing essential resources such as food and energy. Yet, our oceans are under threat from pollution, overfishing, and the impacts of climate change. These challenges endanger marine ecosystems and the livelihoods of billions of people who depend on healthy oceans. Protecting life below water requires global cooperation, responsible management, and a commitment to sustainable practices. By raising awareness, enforcing policies, and taking collective action, we can preserve marine environments for future generations, ensuring the balance of oceanic ecosystems and the services they provide. The goal of the campaign is to end impunity for crimes committed on and against the world’s oceans and their related human rights violations through increased transparency in the fisheries sector and seafood industry.

Reefolution

Reefolution works to restore and protect marine ecosystems, focusing on coral reefs and their vital role in ocean health. Their approach involves the use of innovative techniques such as coral farming, outplanting, and the restoration of degraded reefs to enhance biodiversity and support local economies. By partnering with communities, governments, and scientific organizations, Reefolution is dedicated to ensuring that coral reefs thrive in the face of climate change and human impact. Their efforts include education and advocacy to raise awareness about the importance of marine ecosystems, as well as promoting sustainable practices to safeguard ocean life for future generations.
